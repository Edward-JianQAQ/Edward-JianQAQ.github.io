I am a Ph.D. student in the [Data Systems Group](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-systems-group/) at the [Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/), University of Waterloo, advised by Prof. [M. Tamer Özsu](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~tozsu/). I work on **multimodal and agentic data management**: natural-language interfaces to databases, complex analytical tasks over heterogeneous federated systems, computer-use agents, and multimodal learning.

I was a research intern at **Salesforce AI Research** in 2026 and a visiting researcher at **ServiceNow Research** from 2024 to 2025. Before Waterloo, I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from **City University of Hong Kong**, advised by Prof. [Yu Yang](https://yuyangcs.github.io/), working on data mining and graph learning. Off the clock, I follow [Manchester City](https://www.mancity.com/) closely.
